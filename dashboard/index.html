How the value is worked out

implied = TVL × 0.30 × (1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 100 × 0.60) × (0.70 + min(age, 1460d) ÷ 1460 × 0.50) × (1 + 0.04 × min(chains − 1, 5))

The page recomputes every row from that formula and regenerates every farm plan from the row’s own record, so the numbers on screen are the model running, not a copy of a table. A protocol “records no token” when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is the record’s word, not proof it has none. The board never predicts whether or when a token is issued.
